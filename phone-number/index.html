Helping Your Boyfriend Phone Number

Number players dial in the game

015767438

This is an in-game number for endings 3 and 4. Do not dial it on a real phone. It is not a developer contact and not a helpline.

The digits are not printed in the store description on the official itch.io page. They come from players who finished the later endings, and from a playthrough in which the protagonist reads a number written on their own arm. fluuen's comment on the official page, which other players thanked, tells you to call 015767438 and pick the third choice. Sammi's 4 October 2026 guide tells you to write down the phone number on your arm before the ending. Those two reports name the same clue.

When the number shows up

You will not have a phone puzzle in the opening conversation with Adrian. The number belongs to the later investigation. In the playthrough, the protagonist wakes, sees writing on their arm, and recognizes the shape of a phone number. The handwriting is described as their own. Sammi's instruction to "annotate" it means exactly that: copy the digits while they are on screen, because the call happens in a different scene.

If you skipped the line with history-log or skip keys, open the log with L or reload a save from before that morning. Guessing a similar string will not help. The public reports agree on 015767438, nine digits, not on a shorter or longer variant.

What you need before the call

The number alone does not start ending 3 or ending 4. Players who only memorized it and then stayed on Adrian's good side still report ending 1 or ending 2. The call sits behind three other conditions.

  1. Keep Adrian's affection low enough that, after you use the scalpel, you can take a key. High affection is the ending 2 report, and that report has no key.
  2. Find the address before the call. fluuen says the third phone choice stays locked unless you investigate the package. A reply on the same thread describes a small cardboard box on the bedroom floor during the upstairs search, with the address on it. Sammi places that address in Adrian's room. No public guide prints the street address, so this page does not invent one. Read the package in your own game.
  3. Use the key on the locked door before you leave. Players call it the locked room, the middle door, or the door in front of you. Angeleo says you exit the scalpel room, use the key on the door ahead, and only then call Oliver.

The walkthrough puts these beats in play order. The endings guide compares all four routes. This page only covers the call.

How to place the call

Once the door is open and the address has already been read, dial the number above on the in-game phone. fluuen says to select the third choice after the number. Angeleo describes the same call as reaching Oliver and telling him the address. Oliver is a credited voice role on the official page, played by Omong, which supports the idea that the contact is an in-story character rather than a menu easter egg.

The third choice and the address line up. fluuen warns that you are locked out of that option if you never investigated the package. If the phone offers fewer lines than a guide promised, the missing piece is the address clue, not a different phone number. Go back, inspect the box in Adrian's room, and return to the phone. Do not try extra codes. None of the player guides publish a second number.

Ending 3, ending 4, and the same call

Ending 3 and ending 4 both use the same in-game number. It is not the switch between them. Player guides put the switch on Ian. Low affection with Ian, together with low affection with Adrian, is ending 3. High affection with Ian, still with Adrian low, is ending 4. Jupikoou, Angeleo, fluuen, and Sammi all describe that split, even though some of them spell Adrian as Adrien. The route comparison is on the endings guide.

So if the call connects and you still get the ending you did not want, replaying the phone scene will not fix it. Change Ian's earlier conversations, keep the low Adrian value that gave you the key, and place the same call again. If the key is missing, Ian is not the problem yet. Raise or lower Ian only after the key exists.

If the call fails

  • There is no phone yet. You are still early. Finish the night scenes and the investigation before you look for a handset. The walkthrough marks that point without adding fake chapter names.
  • The third choice is missing. Inspect the package or cardboard box in Adrian's room and read the address, then dial again. fluuen ties that choice to the package.
  • You cannot reach the phone because a door is locked. The key is the step before the call. Players only report the key when Adrian's affection is low and the scalpel has been used.
  • You took the key and still reached ending 2. Check that you opened the door before leaving, and that you actually placed the call. Having the key in the scene is not the same as using it.
  • The number on your arm looked broken across two lines. The playthrough reads it as 015767438 once the lines are put together. Dial the full nine digits.
  • You are looking for a real-world phone, a Discord number, or a code for a browser copy. The developers' contact path is their itch.io page. The digits above are a story clue, and this page does not host the game.

What this number is not

The number in the box above is a clue inside a horror visual novel. The game's own warnings include violence, drug use, and a coercive relationship. The phone call is part of that fictional plot. Calling the digits from a real handset can reach an unrelated service, a disconnected line, or a paid number, depending on your country. Do not do it. If you need help because the game's subject matter was distressing, use a local support service you already trust, not a number from the script.

It is also not printed on the store page as an official password, and it is not required to launch the Windows, macOS, or Android download. You can start version 1.0.1 from the official page with no code at all. The number matters only after you are inside the ending 3 and ending 4 investigation.

Sources for the digits

Checked on 11 October 2026. The official page confirms four endings, the character names in the voice cast, and the premise. It does not, in the text reviewed, print 015767438. The digits are corroborated by fluuen's itch.io comment ("Call the number 015767438. Select the third choice."), by Sammi's guide telling players to record the number written on the arm, and by a published playthrough that reads those digits from the arm. Angeleo's comment supplies the Oliver-and-address description of the same call. Use the in-game writing if your copy shows a different string. A future patch could change a clue, and a fan page should not override the text on your screen.
