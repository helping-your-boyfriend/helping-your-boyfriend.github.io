Helping Your Boyfriend Endings

This page spells out how players reach all four endings. Stop here if you want to discover the late-night choices yourself.

The official itch.io page says there are four endings and does not print a chart. The table is from player reports. Where those reports disagree, both wordings stay in the sections below. This page does not add a fifth ending or an official title the game does not print.

All four endings at a glance

Ending Affection players track Reported late actions
Ending 1 Sammi: Adrian high, Ian low. Described as a death. Refuse Ian's pill, or take the blue pill, pretend to swallow, and do not use the scalpel. Sammi also lists taking Adrian's pill and not using the scalpel.
Ending 2 Adrian high. Ian low in Sammi's guide. Angeleo suggests Ian may also need to stay low. Take Ian's pill and do not keep Adrian's pill down. Use the scalpel. Do not take the key.
Ending 3 Adrian low and Ian low. Same pill handling as ending 2, then use the scalpel, take the key, open the locked door, and place the call.
Ending 4 Adrian low and Ian high. Same key, door, and call as ending 3. Ian's affection is the reported difference.

The play order for the key, the door, and the call is in the walkthrough. The digits and the failed-call checks are on the phone number page.

Sammi's guide also says that game-over screens exist apart from these four endings. Dying during a minigame or a quick-time event is a failed attempt, not ending 1. Reload and try the prompt again, or lower the difficulty added in version 1.0.1, before you change a story choice.

Ending 1

Players describe ending 1 as the outcome in which you die. It is the route people often hit when they side with Adrian and do not follow the later escape steps. Sammi lists high affection with Adrian, low affection with Ian, taking Adrian's pill, refusing Ian's pill, and refusing the scalpel.

fluuen describes two ways into the same ending. One is simply not taking the blue pill. The other is taking the blue pill, pretending to swallow, and then refusing the scalpel when it is offered. Those two reports do not use the same noun for the pill. Sammi separates "Ian's pill" from "Adrian's pill." fluuen says "blue pill." If the buttons on your screen do not use those exact words, follow the action the guides agree on for this death: you do not complete the path that both keeps Ian's dose and then uses the scalpel.

Jupikoou's short comment reduces ending 1 to "you die," which matches the other reports on the outcome and adds no extra steps. None of these sources gives this ending a formal name.

Ending 2

Ending 2 is the report players give when Adrian's affection stays high, the scalpel is used, and the key is never taken. Sammi's conditions are high Adrian affection, low Ian affection, taking Ian's pill, and not taking Adrian's pill. Angeleo, answering someone who already had endings 1, 3, and 4, says to keep Adrien's affection high, possibly keep Ian's low, take Ian's pill, spit out Adrien's pill, and stab without turning around for the key. fluuen's version of the same branch is: take the blue pill, pretend to swallow, and use the scalpel.

The useful test, if you are reloading, is whether a key prompt appears after the scalpel. On the high-Adrian reports, it does not. Angeleo specifically says you will not turn around to get the key. If the key prompt is missing, you are not on ending 3 or ending 4 yet, even if the scalpel scene felt similar.

Ending 3

Ending 3 uses the locked door and the phone, with both affection values kept low. Sammi says Adrian's affection and Ian's affection both need to be low, that you take Ian's pill and not Adrian's, and that a low enough Adrian value makes a key available on his body. You then enter the locked room before going outside. Sammi also tells you to write down the phone number on your arm and the address found in Adrian's room before you need them.

fluuen requires low affection with both characters, the blue-pill sequence, the scalpel, the middle door, the number 015767438, and the third phone choice. That comment says the third choice is unavailable unless you investigate the package. Angeleo describes taking the key after the scalpel, using it on the door in front of you, calling Oliver, and telling him the address, then going out to find Ian. Jupikoou says the same shape: both affections low, a key if Adrian's hatred is low enough, then the phone, the number, and the address.

Read those as one route told four ways. The door is called "locked," "middle," or "the door in front of you," depending on the writer. The call is covered in full on the phone number page, including why 015767438 should not be dialed on a real phone. Ending 3 is the low-Ian version of that call. Raising Ian's affection instead is what players change for ending 4.

Ending 4

Every detailed report says ending 4 repeats the ending 3 actions and changes Ian. Sammi: Adrian low, Ian high, Ian's pill taken, Adrian's pill refused, key taken, locked room entered before you go outside, phone number and address recorded. fluuen: low affection with Adrien, high affection with Ian, then the same blue pill, scalpel, middle door, 015767438, and third choice, still depending on the package. Angeleo: the ending 3 steps, but Ian's affection high. Jupikoou: the ending 3 steps, with Ian's affection as high as possible.

If you already earned ending 3, do not rebuild the phone puzzle from scratch. Keep the low Adrian value that produced the key, keep the package investigation that unlocked the address choice, and replay the conversations that raise Ian. A save from before those Ian conversations is more useful than a save made after the scalpel.

Ending 3 and ending 4

The phone number does not choose between these two endings. Players dial 015767438 on both. The key, the locked door, and the address given to Oliver are also shared. Ian's affection is the split that these guides actually describe. Low Ian affection is ending 3. High Ian affection is ending 4. Adrian stays low on both, because a high Adrian value is what blocks the key in the ending 2 reports. How to place that call is on the phone number page.

A common miss is doing the Ian half correctly and still never seeing the key. That usually means Adrian's affection was not low enough. Another miss is seeing the key and the door, then finding no usable phone choice. fluuen ties that missing choice to skipping the package. The address itself is not printed in any of these public guides, so this page will not invent one. You read it in the game, from the package or box players describe in Adrian's room.

Where the guides disagree

The official page and the voice cast spell the doctor Adrian. Sammi uses Adrian and, once, Adrien. fluuen and Angeleo write Adrien. Those are spelling differences in player posts, not two characters. Ian and Oliver are separate people: both have credits in the voice cast, Ian as BigG and Oliver as Omong. Jupikoou calls Adrian and Ian brothers. The short store description does not. Treat that family wording as part of the player guide, not as a line copied from the store page.

"Pretend to swallow," "spit it out," and "don't take Adrian's pill" are probably attempts to describe one on-screen choice, but the public comments do not include a screenshot of the button. If your label matches none of them, use the result the writers are aiming at: Adrian's dose does not stay down, Ian's dose is the one you follow, and the scalpel decision comes after that. Do not stack every verb from every comment in one playthrough.

Questions about the endings

How do I get ending 3?

Keep affection low with both Adrian and Ian, follow Ian's pill rather than Adrian's, use the scalpel, take the key, open the locked door, and complete the call with the address. The number and the package step are on the phone number guide.

How do I get ending 4?

Use the ending 3 key, door, and call, but keep Ian's affection high while Adrian's stays low. If the key never appears, Adrian's value is the one players say is still too high.

Why do I only get ending 1 or ending 2?

Ending 1 is the death report, often from refusing the scalpel path or from taking Adrian's side of the pill choice. Ending 2 is the high-Adrian scalpel path without the key. To leave those two, the reports say you have to lower Adrian's affection before the late scene, not only change the final button.

Are there more than four endings?

The official page says four. Player guides add game-over screens when a minigame or quick-time event fails. Those are retries, not extra endings. This guide does not document any ending beyond those four.
