How to Play Helping Your Boyfriend Online

Helping Your Boyfriend is a cute-turned-creepy horror visual novel. Select Play Now above to load it in the browser. A Windows, macOS, or Android download is listed below if you want a local build. The official control list covers reading and the minigames. The evening order, saves, and late choices are in the walkthrough.

  • Left click or Enter advances the text and confirms a choice.
  • The mouse is used to point, click, and drag inside minigames.
  • Space hides or shows the dialogue box, which is useful when you want to see the illustration.
  • L opens the history log if you click past a line.
  • A toggles auto-play.
  • Hold Ctrl, or press Tab, to skip text you already know.
  • Arrow keys and the spacebar are used for quick-time events.
  • The bell at the top opens the in-game menu, including Save, Load, and Settings.

Version 1.0.1, announced in the 2 October 2026 devlog, adds a gameplay difficulty setting. The devlog does not publish a chart of what each difficulty changes. If a timed prompt is blocking you, change that setting or reload a save. A failed minigame is not one of the four story endings. The store page says there are five minigames and does not name them. Dragging with the mouse is the published minigame control. The doll work in the story is the recurring job, not a guarantee that every minigame is the same puzzle.

A keyboard and mouse match the published controls. An Android build is a real download, and quick-time events on the written control list still use arrow keys and space.

Helping Your Boyfriend Endings

The official page says the game has four endings. It does not print a route chart. Player reports line up on a broad split. Ending 1 is described as a death. Ending 2 keeps affection toward Adrian high, uses the scalpel, and does not take a key. Endings 3 and 4 need Adrian's affection low enough to take that key, then a locked door and a phone call. Ian's affection is the reported difference: low for ending 3, high for ending 4. The comparison, including where the pill labels disagree, is on the endings guide. Sammi also separates ordinary game-over screens from the four endings.

Helping Your Boyfriend Phone Number

Players who reached endings 3 and 4 report dialing 015767438 inside the game. A playthrough describes those digits written on the protagonist's arm. Helping Your Boyfriend FAQ

What kind of game is Helping Your Boyfriend?

The creator lists a visual novel made in Unity with Naninovel. The itch.io page tags it as a visual novel and as survival, with horror, romance, thriller, psychological horror, point-and-click, and 2D art. You download it for Windows, macOS, or Android.

How many endings are there?

The official page says four endings. Player guides also mention separate game-over screens from failed minigames. The route split is on the endings page.

What should I download?

Use version 1.0.1 from the official itch.io page: the PC zip, the Mac zip, or the Android apk. That update adds the difficulty option.

Who is Adrian?

Adrian is the doctor boyfriend named in the store description and in the voice cast, where Vertin Vii is credited in the role. Some player posts spell the name Adrien. This guide uses Adrian, the spelling on the official page.

Is the frame on this page a working game?

Select Play Now and the frame loads the browser game. If it stays blank, use the Windows, macOS, or Android download on the official itch.io page.

About this guide

This is an independent fan guide. It is not the official website, and it is not written by the developers. Zieelane is credited as project manager, lead writer, programmer, and UI designer. Mousefishdog is credited for characters and CG art. The official page also lists music, sound design, an English translation credit, and voice roles for Adrian, Ian, and Oliver. The project was submitted to the Spooktober 8th Annual Visual Novel Jam.

You play as Adrian's partner. Adrian is described as a sweet, caring doctor. A leg illness has left your character unable to walk, so you have moved in with him. You wake late at night, around the time he gets home from work, and the job he gives you is taking old dolls apart. The store text then turns. One night a stranger pins you down, covers your mouth, and holds out a pill with the line "Take this, or you die."

The same page lists four endings, five minigames, more than 8,000 English words, a Thai script, 115 CGs and character sprites, and 30 backgrounds. Before the story starts you can customize your name, pronouns, and body type. A launch trailer on the ZieelaneGames YouTube channel is dated 30 September 2026. Checked on 11 October 2026, the itch.io page listed the game as Released and showed version 1.0.1 downloads. The store page says an average session is about half an hour. Players who retry minigames or hunt the other endings spend longer. One comment on the official page said the game kept an automatic save before quick-time events. That is one player's report. Still make your own saves.

Content warnings

The official warning list is specific. Helping Your Boyfriend includes jumpscares, loud noises, flashing and disturbing images, blood and cartoon gore, violence, drug use, unhealthy and obsessive behavior, toxic relationships, and crude language. The bright presentation does not remove those elements. Set the volume before the first evening scene, and leave if the material stops being something you want to watch.

The story's dependence is part of the premise: your character cannot walk, lives with a doctor boyfriend, and is asked to take a pill under threat. That is horror fiction. Nothing on this site is advice about real medical care, real relationships, or real medication.
