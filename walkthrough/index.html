Helping Your Boyfriend Walkthrough

This walkthrough is for the downloadable game on itch.io, version 1.0.1 when this page was written. It follows the official setup and controls first, then the late choices players actually use. It does not rename scenes the store page never named, and it does not list five minigames by invented titles. Route spoilers are marked before they start.

Before you install

Helping Your Boyfriend is a horror visual novel. Zieelane and Mousefishdog ship it as a name-your-own-price download for Windows, macOS, and Android. The files listed with the 1.0.1 update are a PC zip, a Mac zip, and an Android apk. Download them from the official itch.io page. This fan site does not host the game.

Read the warnings before the first evening. The official list includes jumpscares, loud noises, flashing and disturbing images, blood and cartoon gore, violence, drug use, unhealthy obsessive behavior, toxic relationships, and crude language. The developers say the story is fictional and tell you to stop if it is too much. Headphones make the loud cues harsher. Turn the volume down in the bell menu before you need that advice.

The English script is more than 8,000 words, and the game can also be played in Thai. itch.io lists an average session of about half an hour. Plan on longer if you retry minigames or hunt more than one of the four endings. A recorded all-endings sitting runs past an hour. That is a viewing length, not a rule for your own pace.

First launch

Choose the language you want to read, then set the protagonist's name, pronouns, and body type. Those options are on the official feature list. They change how the lead is addressed and shown. They are not a difficulty mode and not a hidden ending flag in any player guide used here.

Open the bell at the top of the screen before the first choice. That menu is Save, Load, and Settings. Make one save as soon as the story lets you, and keep it. Version 1.0.1 adds a gameplay difficulty option. The devlog from 2 October 2026 announces the option without a public table of every timing change. If a later quick-time event is only failing because of speed, change difficulty there instead of rewriting your dialogue choices.

One player on the official comments said the game had already saved automatically before a quick-time event. That report is welcome, and it is still one player's run. Keep a manual slot anyway. The bell menu is the method the store page documents.

Controls you will actually use

  • Left click or Enter moves the text forward and confirms a choice.
  • The mouse points, clicks, and drags when a minigame replaces the dialogue.
  • Space shows or hides the text box. During a quick-time event, space is also an action key, so look at the prompt before you assume it only hides text.
  • L opens the history log. Use it after an accidental click, not as an undo button. The log does not rewind a choice.
  • A toggles auto-play. Leave it off while you are reading a scene for the first time.
  • Hold Ctrl, or press Tab, to skip lines you already know on a replay. Release skip before a new choice.
  • Arrow keys plus spacebar cover quick-time events.

The store page says the game contains five minigames and does not name them. Do not search this walkthrough for a fifth puzzle title that was never published. When the picture changes from a conversation to a workspace, stop advancing text and read the instruction on that screen. Dragging is the mouse action the official controls mention. Follow the tool the current minigame highlights. A failure there is a game over in Sammi's wording, separate from the four story endings, so reload rather than treating it as a route.

The opening routine

The store description places you in Adrian's home after a leg illness leaves you unable to walk. You wake late at night, near the time he returns from work. The job he brings home is taking old dolls apart, and that job becomes the ordinary evening. Play those scenes in the order the game offers them. Click through conversations with purpose: the later guides track affection, and agreeable lines with Adrian are what players say push you toward ending 1 and ending 2.

You do not need the phone number during this stretch. If you came here only for 015767438, it is a later clue, explained on the phone number page. Writing it down early will not unlock a door that the story has not opened.

Adrian is the spelling on the official page and in the voice cast. Some walkthroughs write Adrien. Ian and Oliver are the other credited speaking roles. A player guide calls Adrian and Ian brothers. The store blurb does not, so this walkthrough does not treat that relationship as required knowledge for the buttons you press.

The night the pill appears

Spoilers from here through the end of the page. The official teaser already says a stranger pins you down and demands that you take a pill or die. The steps below go further.

Make a fresh save before you commit to the pill and the scalpel. Player routes divide on three things: whose pill you follow, whether you use the scalpel, and whether Adrian's affection is low enough to reveal a key afterward. Sammi separates Ian's pill from Adrian's pill. fluuen talks about a blue pill and a "pretend to swallow" action. Angeleo says to take Ian's pill and spit Adrian's out. Those phrases are trying to describe the same late choice. Use the words printed on your buttons. The result the ending hunters want, for every route except the death, is that Ian's dose is the one you follow and Adrian's dose does not stay down.

Refusing the scalpel is the report for ending 1, which players call a death. Using the scalpel while Adrian's affection is still high is the report for ending 2, and those players do not get the key. The full split is in the endings guide. You can finish a first run on purpose without optimizing it. A save from before the pill is what makes the second run short.

Affection on a replay

The game does not, in the public store text, show a numeric formula for affection. Players still talk about it as something you can raise or lower through earlier conversations, and their ending results line up often enough to plan around it.

  • Ending 1 and ending 2 reports keep Adrian's affection high. Ending 2 also keeps Ian's affection low.
  • Ending 3 reports keep both values low.
  • Ending 4 reports keep Adrian low and Ian high.

If you want ending 3 or ending 4, do not spend the early evenings only choosing the warmest reply to Adrian. Players who did that describe getting stuck on the first two endings even after they learned the phone number. Keep two saves if you can: one early save for a different Adrian approach, and one later save once his affection is already low, so you can change only Ian.

There is no published point value that counts as "low enough." The practical test players use is the key. After the scalpel, look for a chance to take a key from Adrian. Angeleo says that chance appears when you can interact with him on the low-affection route, and that the high-affection route does not turn you back toward the key. If the prompt is absent, reload an earlier save and cool the Adrian conversations. Do not keep replaying the scalpel on the same high-affection file.

The locked room, the package, and the phone

Ending 3 and ending 4 share this sequence in the player reports. Take the key. Leave the scalpel room. Open the locked door in front of you, also called the middle door. Inside the investigation, read the address. Then dial the in-game number.

The address is the easy clue to miss. fluuen says the third phone choice never appears if you skip the package. Another comment describes a small cardboard box on the bedroom floor while you are searching upstairs, with the address printed on it. Sammi says the address is in Adrian's room and tells you to write it down beside the number from your arm. No guide publishes the address string. Copy what your game prints. Then use 015767438, which players identify as the number on the arm, and give Oliver that address. The third menu choice is the line fluuen tells you to pick, and it matches Angeleo's "tell him the address." The digits, the warning not to dial them for real, and the failed-call checks are on the phone number guide.

Do this before you walk out. Sammi's guide says to enter the locked room before going outside. Opening the door after you have already left is not the order these write-ups describe. If you are outside with no call behind you, reload to the key.

Ian's affection does not change the number you dial. It changes which of the two call endings you get. Low Ian affection is ending 3. High Ian affection is ending 4. Both still need the low Adrian value, the key, the package, and the same phone number. The split between those two endings is on the endings guide.

How to replay without starting over

  1. Protect an early save and a save from just before the pill.
  2. After any ending, load the early save if you need to change Adrian's affection, or the later save if only Ian needs to change.
  3. Skip familiar text with Ctrl or Tab. Turn auto-play off again when a choice or a minigame appears.
  4. On the low-Adrian file, confirm the key exists before you worry about the phone.
  5. Inspect the package even if you think you remember the address. The choice can stay locked when the inspection was skipped, not only when the digits were wrong.
  6. Dial 015767438 and use the address option. Then check Ian's affection if the ending is the wrong one of the pair.

Skip keys make a second run much shorter than the first. They also make it easy to miss the arm writing or the box. If a clue is gone, the history log is the first place to look. A missing choice usually means the inspection never happened, which the log cannot retroactively perform. Reload.

What this walkthrough will not invent

The official page does not name the five minigames, does not print a map, and does not list chapter titles. A Thai playthrough video uses day markers, but those are chapter labels on that video, not a table of contents shipped with the game. This page therefore stays with phases you can recognize: setup, the doll routine, the pill night, the affection replay, and the key-door-phone ending. If a guide elsewhere names a puzzle this page does not, check it against your own screen before you follow it.

Setup and the official download stay on the homepage. When you want the four-ending comparison, use the endings guide. When you want the digits, the third choice, and the reasons a call fails, use the phone number guide.
